# Lishan Foundation

> Building open technology, tools, and research for the public good.

## About Us
Lishan Foundation is an open, global non-profit initiative dedicated to developing accessible software, open research, and community-driven solutions.

## Mission
- Democratize access to powerful technology and open-source tools.
- Foster international collaboration between developers and researchers.
- Solve real-world practical challenges without commercial paywalls.
